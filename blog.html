For this individual project, I decided to challenge myself by integrating the Bootstrap 5 framework rather than relying purely on vanilla CSS. Overcoming Bugs: A Problem-Solving Journey

While constructing the contact page, my responsive navigation menu suddenly broke and refused to toggle on small viewports. Instead of panicking, I systematically debugged the issue: analyzing script execution and inspecting dependencies. I discovered a missing Bootstrap bundle script link in the head element. Fixing this minor bug was a great lesson in technical resilience and attention to detail.
